Isolation: two tenants share the address 10.50.50.11, each has its own VNI.

Tenant alpha (VNI 1000) uses 02:00:03:e8:.. on 10.50.50.11
Tenant beta  (VNI 2000) uses 02:00:07:d0:.. on 10.50.50.11

a1 pings alpha's 10.50.50.11 — its ARP shows 03:e8 (alpha's MAC). b2 (beta's
VM with the SAME IP) captures nothing: alpha's frames never cross into beta's
VNI. b1 then pings beta's 10.50.50.11 and its ARP shows 07:d0 (beta's MAC):
same address, different MAC, different tenant. VXLAN's VNI keeps them apart.
