Flood-and-learn: hv3's VTEP table learns a1's MAC from the flooded copy.

Setup: tenant alpha (VNI 1000), VMs a1 (hv1), a2 (hv2), a3 (hv3). The ping is
a1 -> a2, crossing hv1/hv2. hv3 hosts a3 but neither end of the ping, so
anything hv3 learns about a1 must have arrived as a flooded copy
(head-end replication: the 00:00:00:00:00:00 dst 10.50.0.x permanent entries
below are the pre-programmed flood destinations).

Key proof: at the bottom, "hv3 fdb after" contains "02:00:03:e8:00:0a dst
10.50.0.1" — that's a1's MAC (VNI 1000 in the middle of the MAC) learned on
hv3, which has no alpha VM. Only the flooded copy of a1's traffic could teach
it. (The tcpdump 0-packet count is a capture-timing artefact; the FDB is the
authoritative record.)
